# BMS Care Connect

Project Type:
Professional Non-Emergency Medical Transportation (NEMT) Website




Business Name:
BMS Transportation




Industry:
Non-Emergency Medical Transportation (NEMT)




Primary Goal:
Generate calls and bookings for NEMT services while establishing trust, professionalism, and medical credibility.




⸻




🎨 BRANDING & DESIGN
	•	Primary Colors: Orange and White
	•	Accent Colors: Black or dark gray for text contrast
	•	Overall Style: Clean, modern, professional, medical-grade
	•	Vibe: Trustworthy, reliable, compassionate, efficient
	•	Logo: I will upload the BMS Transportation logo separately — design the site to match it perfectly.




Use smooth sections, rounded cards, subtle shadows, strong CTAs, and clean spacing. No clutter.




⸻




🧠 TARGET AUDIENCE
	•	Patients needing transportation to medical appointments
	•	Seniors and individuals with disabilities
	•	Dialysis patients
	•	Hospitals, clinics, rehab centers, nursing homes
	•	Care coordinators and case managers




Assume users want quick answers, easy booking, and clear trust signals.




⸻




🏠 HOMEPAGE (LANDING PAGE STYLE)




The homepage should act as a high-converting landing page, not overly wordy.




Hero Section:
	•	Strong headline such as:
“Reliable, Safe & On-Time Non-Emergency Medical Transportation”
	•	Subheadline:
Serving patients with comfort, care, and professionalism.
	•	Primary CTA buttons:
	•	Call Now
	•	Book a Ride
	•	Include trust icons (insured, professional drivers, on-time service)




⸻




Services Overview Section:




Show clean service cards with icons:
	•	Non-Emergency Medical Transportation (NEMT)
	•	Dialysis Transportation
	•	Doctor & Hospital Appointments
	•	Wheelchair Transportation
	•	Assisted Transportation
	•	Long-Distance Medical Transport




Each service should be short, clear, and reassuring.




⸻




Why Choose BMS Transportation:




Bullet or card style:
	•	Licensed & Insured
	•	Professional, Trained Drivers
	•	Clean, Well-Maintained Vehicles
	•	On-Time & Reliable Service
	•	Patient-First Care
	•	Affordable & Transparent Pricing




⸻




How It Works:
	1.	Book Your Ride
	2.	Get Picked Up On Time
	3.	Arrive Safely & Comfortably




Keep this extremely simple and visual.




⸻




CTA Section:




Big bold section with orange background:
“Need a Ride to Your Medical Appointment?”
Buttons:
	•	Call Now
	•	Schedule Transportation




⸻




📄 ADDITIONAL PAGES




About Us




Explain BMS Transportation’s mission:
	•	Commitment to patient safety
	•	Compassionate service
	•	Reliable medical transportation
Keep it warm and professional.
